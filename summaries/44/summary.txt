Kalkin has been engaged by Zykrr Technologies Private Limited to undertake security testing against the https://insights.zykrr.com application. The Level 1 testing took place over the period from 28-07-2026 to 14-08-2026. During this period the application was analyzed and assessed using a combination of standard tools and utilities and the knowledge and experience of our technical team.

HTTP compression is enabled on sensitive responses, allowing BREACH exploitation. This vulnerability indicates insufficient security controls in the application design and implementation, which may be abused by attackers to compromise confidentiality, integrity, or availability of the system. Secrets in responses may be leaked. leading to potential account or system compromise. Session tokens may be exposed. resulting in loss of sensitive or confidential information. Confidentiality is reduced. which negatively impacts business operations and user trust.

Disable compression for sensitive pages. to reduce the immediate attack surface. Use random padding. to ensure consistent security enforcement. Separate secrets from user input. as part of regular security hardening practices.
