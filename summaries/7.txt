Improperly secured RDP services can reveal system information and increase the risk of brute-force attacks and unauthorized access, especially when Network Level Authentication (NLA) is not enabled.

Restrict RDP access through firewalls or VPNs, enforce Network Level Authentication, use strong credentials, and keep systems updated to reduce the attack surface.
