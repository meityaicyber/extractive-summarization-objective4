Kalkin has been engaged by SMC Global Securities Ltd to undertake security testing against the https://uat-trade.smctradeonline.com web application. The Level 2 testing took place over the period from 24-07-2026 to 10-08-2026. During this period the application was analyzed and assessed using a combination of standard tools and utilities and the knowledge and experience of our technical team.

The report attributes the issue to improper handling of sensitive information in cached client code, suggesting that data is not adequately protected when cached. The X-XSS-Protection header was not configured on the web server or application.

Deploy the X-XSS-Protection header to mitigate XSS attacks.
